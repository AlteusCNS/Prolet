About Conversational Voice Screening

Prolet allows discreet speech motor evaluation in conference settings using natural conversational tasks:

Test Protocols:

  • "Hellooo": Conversational greeting contour measuring prosodic intonation & stability.
  • "Pa-Ta-Ka": Diadochokinesis test measuring oral-motor pacing & coordination.
  • "M-m-m" Humming: A quiet closed-mouth tone ideal for conferences.

All processing occurs locally in your browser. No voice data or recordings are uploaded externally.
